**The solution — before writing a single line of code, write this on paper:**

Every time you get a new problem, spend 5 minutes answering these four questions in plain English:

**One — What are my building blocks?**  
What classes do I need? What does each one represent?

**Two — What does each class own?**  
What fields and methods belong to each class?

**Three — Who controls everything?**  
Usually main. It creates objects and calls methods.

**Four — What's the flow?**  
Step by step what happens from start to finish?
